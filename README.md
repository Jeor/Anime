# Anime

A collection of 114 anime cards: 27 studios and companies, 78 genres and themes, seven decades, Trending and Popular, at **1980 × 1114 pixels**, except the Hentai card, preserved at its supplied **1024 × 576 pixels**.

[Browse the gallery](https://jeor.github.io/Anime/) · [View the full collection](collection.jpg)

Choose a card type or search by card name, then select a card to open its full-resolution image. Cards with two designs use a diagonal split: hover or keyboard-focus either side to expand it, then select that side to open the corresponding image. On touch screens, tap either side directly.

- `cards/`: full-resolution PNG images, including the studio collection in `cards/studios/`
- `alternates/`: nine original designs for the decades, Trending and Popular
- `previews/`: lightweight gallery previews
- `index.html`: responsive, searchable gallery

Anime characters belong to their respective rights holders. This is an unofficial fan collection.
